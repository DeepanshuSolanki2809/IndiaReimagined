Healthcare

A healthy nation is built on accessible healthcare, preventive medicine, medical research, skilled professionals, and modern healthcare infrastructure for every citizen.

Core Pillars

Primary Healthcare

Strengthen community health centres, preventive care, maternal health, and early diagnosis across urban and rural India.

Hospitals

Expand access to modern hospitals equipped with advanced diagnostics, emergency care, and specialized treatment.

Medical Research

Invest in biotechnology, pharmaceutical innovation, genomics, and collaborative research institutions.

Digital Health

Improve healthcare delivery through electronic health records, telemedicine, AI-assisted diagnosis, and secure digital systems.

Future Opportunities

Telemedicine

Increase access to specialist consultations through secure digital healthcare services, especially in remote regions.

AI-Assisted Care

Support clinicians with artificial intelligence for screening, diagnostics, imaging, and workflow improvements.

Mental Health

Expand awareness, counselling services, community support, and accessible mental healthcare across the country.

Emergency Response

Build integrated ambulance networks, trauma centres, and disaster-ready healthcare infrastructure.

Guiding Principles

  • ✔ Healthcare should be accessible to everyone.
  • ✔ Prevention is as important as treatment.
  • ✔ Encourage innovation while protecting patient privacy.
  • ✔ Invest in research, workforce development, and technology.
  • ✔ Build resilient healthcare systems for future generations.
