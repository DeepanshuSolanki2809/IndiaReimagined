About India Reimagined

India Reimagined is an open educational project that explores evidence-based ideas for India's future. It is designed to inspire discussion, encourage learning, and showcase how thoughtful planning, innovation, and collaboration can shape a better tomorrow.

Our Mission

To create one of the most comprehensive, visually engaging, and research-driven websites about India's future. Every topic is presented with optimism, supported by reliable data, and clearly distinguishes current realities from future possibilities.

Our Principles

Evidence-Based

Statistics and information should come from trusted government agencies, academic institutions, and international organizations.

Non-Partisan

The project does not promote or oppose any political party, ideology, or government. It focuses on ideas and learning.

Future-Oriented

We explore opportunities for progress in infrastructure, education, healthcare, sustainability, innovation, and quality of life.

Open Source

The website is intended to grow through transparent development, documentation, and community contributions.

Project Roadmap

Version 1

Core pages, responsive design, reusable components, and GitHub Pages deployment.

Version 2

Interactive maps, searchable content, charts, state profiles, and downloadable resources.

Version 3

Advanced dashboards, multilingual support, richer datasets, and additional educational content.

Data Sources

Planned references include the Government of India, NITI Aayog, MoSPI, RBI, World Bank, WHO, UNESCO, IMF, UNDP, OECD, OpenStreetMap, and other publicly available datasets.

Get Involved

India Reimagined is designed as an evolving knowledge project. Future contributions may include research, design improvements, data visualizations, accessibility enhancements, documentation, and educational resources.
